# jeremiahdaws.com — site brief (redesign-ai)

## Positioning line

**I make films with AI, build tools with AI, and teach people to do both.**

The proof that makes this different from every other "AI consultant" page: he is a
working film director (Producer / Director at FORM, six years producing for Disney,
broadcast editor for Nat Geo and Hallmark). The AI work is credible *because* the
craft came first. Every section should lean on that, not on adjectives.

## Who visits, and what each should do

| Visitor | What they need in 10 seconds | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Ops lead / team manager weighing AI training | What the training is, who it's for, what people walk out able to do, why this person can teach | Send a training inquiry |
| Small-business owner who wants an AI-powered site or tool | Real sites he built for real clients, what the AI actually does on them, how a project works | Send a build inquiry |
| Creative director / film peer judging the AI films | The films, playable immediately, with credits and tools, framed by real production credits | Watch, then look at /work |
| FORM's world (colleagues, clients, Andy's network) | He's FORM's Producer / Director; this is his own side practice, not a job hunt | Nothing to do; don't confuse them |

## Rules the design follows

- Films first because they are the most persuasive thing on the site and nobody else
  in the "AI training" space can show them. Builds second because they are real,
  paid client work. Training third because it borrows its credibility from both.
- No job-hunt signals on the home page: no résumé downloads there, no "available".
- Every claim is checkable. Where a fact is missing (training format, prices, tools on
  one film), the page says less rather than inventing it. Unknowns go to Questions.
- Privacy: household assistants are described by what they do, not whose life they
  run. No kids' details, no addresses, Big Plans stays vague.
- One contact form, one ask. "Work with me" replaces "Hire" (reads as employment).

## Cut or demote

- Résumé downloads on home and /apps (job-hunt signal). Keep the video résumé on /work,
  where crew credits are normal.
- "Digital hospitality" / "my wife's half-finished thoughts" copy on /apps (personal,
  and vague). Replace with what the systems do.
- Tool counts that can't be verified today (AgentStack numbers). Use counts only where
  checked.
- Old components (ChoosePath, Featured, About) stay unused; /maker stays reachable from
  Background only, not in the nav.
- Old location (Buford). Use "Atlanta area".

## Page structure

**Home `/`**
1. Hero: name, positioning line, two sentences of credentials, CTAs "Watch the AI
   films" + "Work with me". Brand strip of his own credits.
2. AI Films: the four shorts, click to play, tools and runtime on each.
3. AI Builds: four builds with what the AI does on each; household assistants as a
   short strip; "How a build works" (scope in writing, fixed price, you own it).
4. AI Training: who it's for, three formats, what people leave with, the teaching
   record behind it (department head, two programs built, 2022–2026) without naming
   the school.
5. Background: credit list.
6. Work with me: services, contact form, socials. No résumés.

**`/work`** — film lane: hero, AI films (compact), credits, archive, FORM CTA. Keep.
**`/apps`** — renamed "AI Builds": rewritten hero (what he builds, for whom, how it
works), product builds, the assistants (privacy-safe), also shipped, build CTA.
**`/maker`** — unchanged, past tense, demoted (not in nav).
**No `/training` page yet.** There aren't enough confirmed facts (formats, pricing,
past sessions) to fill one honestly. Revisit once Jeremiah confirms the offers.
