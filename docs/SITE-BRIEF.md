# jeremiahdaws.com — site brief (redesign-ai)

Revised 2026-10-31 after Jeremiah's direction: "really want to present myself as an
AI filmmaker. Less AI training. Mostly just a consultant on other people's AI films."

## Positioning line

**I make films with AI, and help other people make theirs.**

Identity: AI filmmaker. Credential: Producer / Director at FORM, six years producing
for Disney, editor for Nat Geo and Hallmark. The craft came first; that is what makes
the AI films and the consulting credible. Every section leans on that.

## Who visits, and what each should do

| Visitor | What they need in 10 seconds | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Creative director / agency producer with an AI film to make | The films are good; this person can direct and finish; what he'd do on their project | Send a project note |
| Indie filmmaker or brand making a first AI short | What help exists (notes, hands-on, training), and that he's approachable | Send a project note |
| Film-industry peer judging the work | Playable films, real frames, a credible process, real credits | Watch; maybe look at /work |
| FORM's world | He's FORM's Producer / Director; consulting is his own side work | Nothing; don't confuse them |

## Rules

- The films are the hero: a real frame from a film above the fold, the player right
  after, then how they're made.
- Consulting on other people's AI films is the only offer on the home page. Training is
  one line inside it (one-on-one, $100/hour, his stated rate).
- AI builds (/apps) stay live but leave the home flow and the top nav: reachable from
  the Background section, the contact section and the footer.
- No job-hunt signals, no invented clients, testimonials, results or film-consulting
  prices. Unknowns go to Questions.
- Privacy: no kids' details, no addresses; Big Plans stays vague (still = a baseball
  glove, last in the list).
- Real frames, not YouTube title thumbnails.
