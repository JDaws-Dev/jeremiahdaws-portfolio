Halloween night, 1996. Tyler won't take his little brother trick-or-treating, so seven-year-old Joey draws a face on a paper bag and goes out alone. A 104-second horror short made entirely with AI: no camera, no cast. Every shot generated in Seedance 2.5, voices and sound in ElevenLabs, cut and graded in DaVinci Resolve.
