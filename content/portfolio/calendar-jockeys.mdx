I direct Calendar Jockeys, a short animated comedy series from Form Films. Gus and Wendell co-founded a production agency, and they'd make great things all the time if the meetings ever stopped. Calendar jockeys are "the people between you and the work": fluent in follow-up, allergic to decisions. The running joke is that the meeting was the work.
