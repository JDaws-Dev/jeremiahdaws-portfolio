A satisfying VCR ASMR toy. It's Christmas 1992 in the family den: slide a VHS tape out of its sleeve, push it into the VCR and press play to watch the movie's most famous scene. Real box art, a working remote, and Dad's home tapes, recorded off TV with the commercials still in them. Stack: vanilla JS, Web Audio, YouTube.
