Website and AI assistant for ReadyTote, a moving-tote rental business in Dublin, GA. Customers book reusable totes that are delivered to their door and picked up at the new place. One Claude-powered assistant plays three roles: it answers sizing and pricing questions for visitors, tells customers about their own booking on the order page, and gives the owner a view of the week's runs, inventory and bookings in the admin dashboard.
